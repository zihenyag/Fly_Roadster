# FLY! Roadster

An actual first-person driving game, built around observation, momentum and the cost of flight. Sunlit Pacific roads, pale mineral cliffs, blue water, off-white bodywork and a graphite cockpit. The road owns the screen; UI belongs to the car.

## Color
Warm porcelain #ece9df, cockpit #151c1c, navigation orange #ff703e, sea #6fa5ad, pine #304c44. Orange marks the prescribed route and propulsion; danger is never communicated through floating car alerts.

## Typography
Condensed industrial headings (Arial Narrow / sans-serif); Chinese PingFang SC; tabular monospace for live telemetry. Large speed readout, compact plain labels, no ornamental glass cards.

## Layout
Real 3D windshield and cockpit, three independent rear-view renders. Driver instrument screen behind steering wheel; separate central navigation screen. Only the thin top status rail and a bottom control legend sit outside the cockpit.

## Components
Start/pause/result overlays use warm paper, sharp fine rules, generous typography and orange primary actions. WASD and arrow keys are interchangeable for driving. Space / right Ctrl lift, either Shift grips; propulsion has one fixed output without a hold ramp. E / Esc pause. Every other key pauses; resume is mouse-only. No music. Automatic orientation assistance never chooses heading or landing position.
